## Delivered

- `PATCH .../blueprint/draft` edits reviewed candidates with `expectedVersion`, protects
  server-owned identity fields, revalidates the complete draft and recomputes `draftHash`.
- `POST .../blueprint/budget` only raises paused budgets, preserves checkpoints and
  re-enqueues the same durable job.
- `POST .../blueprint/cancel` cancels only the allowed lifecycle states and retains the
  candidate and checkpoint.
- `POST .../blueprint/confirm` requires the current version/hash, complete
  acknowledgements and `confirmed: true`.
- Confirmation re-runs the shared core validation for scale, required fields, forbidden
  elements and outline reference integrity. Blank story-bible cards are not confirmable.
- Confirmation atomically writes locked story-bible and domain-setting records, the
  five-level outline, planned chapters and scene cards, version snapshots and the final
  `blueprint_version`. It does not create `chapter_versions`.
- Formal IDs are generated by the API; draft IDs are not persisted as business IDs.
- Retry, budget update, patch, cancel and confirm write audit records. Retry serializes
  against cancel through a row-locked transaction.
